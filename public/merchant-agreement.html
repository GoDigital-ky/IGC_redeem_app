Merchant Service Agreement

AGREED TERMS:

1. Definitions

For the purpose of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them below:

  • “Services” refers to the listing, promotion, sale, and facilitation of gift cards or vouchers through the Island Gift Cards platform.
  • “Gift Card” or “Voucher” means a prepaid, digital certificate issued by the Merchant and offered through Island Gift Cards, redeemable for goods and/or services up to the value stated therein.
  • “Customer” or “End User” refers to the individual or entity who purchases a gift card through the Island Gift Cards platform or receives it as a recipient.
  • “Pre-agreed Fee” refers to the fee(s) mutually agreed upon by the Parties, as set out in the commercial proposal or offer accepted by the Merchant.
  • “Redeemed” or “Redemption” refers to the use of a valid gift card by the Customer with the Merchant as a form of payment for goods or services.
  • “Commission” means the percentage or fixed fee deducted by Island Gift Cards from the total value of each gift card sale as remuneration for its Services, as mutually agreed upon with each Merchant.
  • “Merchant” refers to the business entity signing this Agreement to list and sell their gift cards through the platform.
